# AX-1 milk system clogging

Known issue on the Autoflow AX-1 bean-to-cup machine: the milk line clogs when the daily rinse is skipped.
Autoflow GmbH (https://autoflow.example/service) ships a revised milk pump (part SKU-40707, "MP-7") with a 42-day
lead time. Autoflow charges USD 3.2k per batch of 10 pumps; the next batch ships in Q1 2027.

## Workaround

Run the automatic rinse every 4 hours and descale weekly. Customers on CareService plans get the
MP-7 pump free of charge ([[service-plans]]). Affected accounts include Meridian Airports Lounge
([[meridian-lounge-escalation]]) and Granite & Grain Diner (TCK-3101, urgent).
