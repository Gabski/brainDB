# 2027 price list

Espresso machines go up 5% on 1 Jan 2027: PX-200 from €7,900 to €8,300 and PX-300 from €10,900 to €11,450.
Grinders, filters and service plans keep their 2026 prices (see pricing.csv).

## Discounts

- enterprise: 12%, needs sales director approval;
- mid-market: 8%;
- smb: list price.

Orders placed before 31.12.2026 keep the 2026 price. The new list applies from Q1 2027 (2027-01-01).
