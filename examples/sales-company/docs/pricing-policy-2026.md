## Discount rules

Discount rules (since the deal desk change in November 2026):

- up to 10%: the account executive decides;
- above 10%: deal desk review and CFO Ilse Varga's sign-off within 48 hours;
- multi-year discounts: 3% for a 2-year term, 6% for a 3-year term, only with the price locked for the term;
- never below the 32% margin floor. Gross margin on pack subscriptions is 41% at list price.

The old rule that let regional managers approve up to 15% is withdrawn.
