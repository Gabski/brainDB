## Deal review (MEDDICC)

Every opportunity above €50,000 goes through deal review before it can enter commit.
Steps: qualify with MEDDICC (metrics, economic buyer, decision criteria, decision process,
paper process, identified pain, champion, competition) → price check against the 2026 price
list, product-claim check against lab data → deal desk → legal → signature → CRM close. The checklist lives in
deal-review-checklist.md. The CRM opportunity id (OPP-xxxx) must be on every slide and in
every forecast line.

## Forecast categories

- **Commit**: stage 4 (negotiation) or later with a signed mutual action plan and a known paper process.
- **Best case**: commit plus stage 3 (evaluation) deals with a confirmed budget.
- **Pipeline**: everything else that is still open.

The forecast call is every Friday at 09:00 CET. Close dates must be updated in the CRM before the call.
Targets and the current commit are in the CRM forecast view.

## Competing with Grayfield Power

Grayfield Power Ltd sells LFP lithium forklift packs at €159 per pack per month (USD 3.2k per pack per year in the US) but has no certified
SAP connector and no on-site service in the Nordics. Lead with availability (above 99%
across our fleet), sodium-ion safety and cold-store performance, the SAP integration, and total cost
over 3 years. Do not match their price; offer a Telemetry pilot instead.

## Product claims (NaCore 2)

NaCore 2 uses the EL-7 electrolyte developed with the Corvane Institute lab. The draft spec sheet says
3,000 cycles to 80% capacity at 25 °C, an operating range of −20 to 45 °C, and 3.7 V nominal / 4.3 V max
per cell. Until the lab's pouch-cell go/no-go on 15 Dec 2026:

- quote cycle life only for 25 °C operation;
- give no cycle-life guarantee above 35 °C (warm zones such as bakeries and fryer halls);
- for cold stores, quote usable capacity at −20 °C only from the lab's measured value;
- every product claim in a proposal cites the lab run id (RUN-xxxx).

## Renewals

Start renewals 120 days before the contract end. Bring the exec sponsor in for any account
above €250,000 ARR. Keep SLA credits inside the renewal (as credit on the new term) rather
than paying them out.
